Privacy Notice and Terms of Use

Draft version 2 · 13 October 2026 · This page describes the planned design of the helpdesk. The sample website itself stores nothing.

The UC-PnC Student Helpdesk answers questions from the University's official manuals. It is a research prototype built for an undergraduate thesis. This notice explains what information the helpdesk keeps about you, why, how it is protected, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Summary

What we keep
Your student number, your password (scrambled, so no one can read it), when you agreed to this notice, and when you sign in.
What we don't keep
Your questions, the answers, your name, contact details or location.
Why
So only UC-PnC students and personnel can use the helpdesk, and misuse can be traced. We use it only with your consent, and never in the study's results.
Where and how
On the helpdesk computer only, in a local database that only the researchers can open. Nothing is sent over the internet.
Who can see it
Only the researchers.
How long
Until the study ends, then it is permanently deleted.
Your rights
See, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or complain to the National Privacy Commission.

3Why we collect itSec. 16(b)(2), Sec. 12(a)

  • To let only registered UC-PnC students and personnel use the helpdesk, because the manuals are meant for the university community.
  • To keep a record of who used the helpdesk and when, so that misuse can be traced.
  • To keep proof that you gave your consent.

Legal basis: your consent. You give it by ticking the box and choosing “Agree and continue” before your account is made.

Your information is not used to grade you, is not sold or shared, and is not part of the study's results: the helpdesk is tested with a fixed set of questions written by the researchers. Because your questions are not saved, they are never used to train the AI model.

4How your information is handledSec. 16(b)(3), (5)

  1. Creating an account. You read this notice and agree; the helpdesk saves the consent record. You choose a password; only its hash is saved.
  2. Signing in. The password you type is checked against the hash and then discarded. A session starts and the sign-in time is saved.
  3. Asking a question. Your question goes from the browser to the helpdesk program on the same computer. The AI model, which also runs on that computer, writes the answer from the manuals. The question is kept in memory only until the answer is made, and is not written to any file or log.
  4. Signing out. The session ends. It also ends by itself after a period of no activity (see section 6).

No other system or program can access your information automatically.

5Who can see itSec. 16(b)(4)

Only the researchers, as the helpdesk's administrators. Your information is not shared with anyone else, inside or outside the University, unless the law requires it (for example, a court order).

6How we protect itSec. 20(a)–(c), (e)

The law asks for organizational, physical and technical measures. These are ours.

Technical

Passwords are hashed, never stored

Each password is turned into a hash with Argon2id, using its own random salt, at or above the minimum settings of the OWASP Password Storage Cheat Sheet (19 MiB of memory, 2 iterations, 1 degree of parallelism). A hash cannot be turned back into the password. The password itself is never saved or written to a log.

The database stays on one computer

Accounts, consent records and sign-in records are kept in a local database on the helpdesk computer. That computer is protected by its own sign-in, and only the researchers can open the database.

Nothing leaves the computer

The helpdesk works offline. It accepts connections only from the computer it runs on (127.0.0.1), and the AI model runs on that same computer. Nothing is sent to the internet or to any outside company.

Sessions end on their own

Each sign-in gets a random session code from a cryptographically secure generator, kept in a cookie that web page scripts cannot read (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict). You are signed out when you choose Sign out, and automatically after 15 minutes without activity, so the next person at the kiosk cannot use your account.

Password guessing is slowed down

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, sign-in to that account pauses for 15 minutes. Failed attempts are counted in memory only and are not recorded.

Questions are not kept

Your question stays in memory only while its answer is made. The helpdesk's logs record no question text.

Organizational

  • Only the researchers have administrator access, each with their own administrator account.
  • The researchers keep your information confidential, during and after the study (Sec. 20(e)).
  • The sign-in record is opened only to look into possible misuse.

Physical

  • The database stays on the helpdesk computer. No backups are made, and it is never copied to USB drives, other computers or cloud storage.
  • The helpdesk computer is kept at [location] and is locked when not in use.

7How long we keep itSec. 16(b)(7), Sec. 11(e)

Until the study ends, [date]. Then the database is permanently deleted. No backups or copies are made, so nothing is left behind. If you ask us to delete your account earlier, we delete your information at that time.

11Terms of use

  1. The helpdesk is for UC-PnC students and personnel only. Use one account, and do not share your password or let others use your account.
  2. The manuals are for the university community. Do not copy or share their content outside the University.
  3. Do not type personal information, yours or anyone else's, into your questions.
  4. Answers are written by an AI model from the manuals and show their sources. They may contain mistakes. For important matters, such as grades, fees and deadlines, confirm with the office named in the answer.
  5. Accounts that break these terms may be removed.
